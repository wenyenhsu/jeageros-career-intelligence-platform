# ATS vs Market Fit

ATS (Simplify-style) is per-job JD **literal keyword** matching. Target coverage is about 70%.

Do not mix ATS with:

- SkillSet / SkillAlias
- Market Fit / Resume Gap / Skill Demand
- embeddings or pgvector

Those layers are market skill intelligence. ATS writes scan notes and resume/cover PDFs from keyword literals only.
